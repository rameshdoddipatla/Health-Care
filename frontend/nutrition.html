Diet and nutrition plans

Pick the plan that matches your goal.

Weight loss plan

  • Low-calorie meals designed for effective fat reduction.
  • Focus on portion control and nutrient-dense foods.
  • Includes fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.
  • Avoids processed foods, sugary snacks, and fried items.
  • Encourages drinking plenty of water and staying hydrated.

Muscle gain plan

  • High-protein meals to support muscle repair and growth.
  • Includes chicken, fish, eggs, lentils, and protein shakes.
  • Balanced with complex carbs like oats, rice, and sweet potatoes.
  • Healthy fats from nuts, seeds, and olive oil for energy.
  • Encourages strength training and post-workout nutrition.

Balanced diet

  • Combination of proteins, carbs, and healthy fats.
  • Promotes overall health, energy, and immunity.
  • Includes seasonal fruits, vegetables, dairy, and whole grains.
  • Limits junk food, excess sugar, and unhealthy fats.
  • Supports long-term lifestyle and sustainable eating habits.
