Immediate tips

Try these simple actions now to reduce stress or anxiety.

  • Take 5 slow deep breaths (inhale 4s, hold 4s, exhale 6s).
  • Short walk for 10 minutes in natural light.
  • Grounding: name 5 things you see, 4 you can touch, 3 you hear.
  • Limit caffeine and hydrate with water.

When to seek help

If you have severe symptoms, thoughts of harming yourself, or feel unsafe, reach out immediately.

Emergency

Call your local emergency number or contact a crisis helpline.
